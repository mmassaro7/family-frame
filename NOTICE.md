Based on [photo-frame-dashboard](https://github.com/wesbos/photo-frame-dashboard) by Wes Bos (MIT License, see `LICENSE`).
The dashboard UI (`css/`, most of `js/`) comes from that project. This version drops the chores, home-control and
agent screens, replaces the mock data with live Google Calendar / Google Drive photos / Open-Meteo weather /
a synced shopping list, and adds the `api/` routes for Vercel.
